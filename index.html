How this works & notes

Priority order: ① a parent’s yahrzeit, ② the member’s own birthday, ③ the rotation. Kohanim and Leviim each have their own least-recent rotation; everyone else is one least-recent list.

Shared data: everything lives in one database. The non-regular list and any data update you make is seen by every gabbai who signs in.

Weekly: after Shabbos, once the real aliyos are entered in Shulspace, export the Aliyos report and update it here — the rotation updates itself for everyone.

Mi Sheberach names: taken from the Hebrew name fields in your Shulspace exports. Blanks can be filled by hand on the sheet.
